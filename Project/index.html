Our Story

Egypt's Soul,
Auckland's Kitchen

Egyptian Halal Food was born from a simple dream: to bring the warmth, colour, and depth of Egyptian home cooking to New Zealand. We believe food is more than sustenance — it is memory, identity, and connection.

Every dish is prepared using traditional recipes passed down through generations, using the finest halal-certified ingredients sourced locally in Auckland and imported directly from Egypt.

Halal Certified

All our meat and ingredients are fully halal certified. We are transparent about what goes into every dish.

Family Recipes

Authentic recipes handed down through three generations, unchanged and uncompromised.

Fresh Daily

We prepare everything fresh each morning. No freezing, no shortcuts — just honest cooking.

Warm Welcome

In Egypt, guests are treated like royalty. We carry that tradition into every visit you make.
